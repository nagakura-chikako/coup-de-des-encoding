# Migration note: level-0 1.1.0 → 2.0.0

Dataset v1.1 → **v2.0** · repository release 1.0.2 → **2.0.0** · schema 0.3 → **0.4** · citation scheme **v1.0, unchanged**.

Level-0 2.0.0 has **224 units** (v1.1: 217). Seven units were split in two; sixteen units had their recorded face corrected. No text was added, removed or altered: every split unit's text equals its two successors joined by one space.

## Why this is a MAJOR change

`docs/05-versioning.md` defines MAJOR as a change to the meaning of an existing field or to the unit-extent rule. The schema used to state, as `id_assignment_rules[1]`, that a line crossing the gutter is one unit recorded on its starting face. Level-0 2.0.0 replaces that rule: a line that crosses the gutter is recorded as one unit per face, whether the two faces are a single sheet or two. That is a change to the unit-extent rule.

The classification was itself revised. On the morning of 2026-11-24 the editor ruled the change MINOR, on the understanding that it only corrected an uneven application of the existing principle "units are cut by physical layout" (the separated pairs of f8–f9 were already split; the crossing lines of f12–f13 and f16–f17 were not). That afternoon, while the schema was being revised, the written rule was found, and the editor re-ruled the change MAJOR. Both rulings are kept in `schemas/level0_schema.json` (`resolutions` › `oq_l0_cross_gutter_units` › `version_classification`).

Each retired identifier stays in its record's `deprecated_ids`, with `superseded_by` listing both successors in reading order. The split positions were checked against the physical Ronat–Papp copy by the editor on 2026-11-24.

Each successor's `note` names its counterpart: the observation that the two fragments form one physical line belongs to level-0. Whether and how they are joined syntactically belongs to the syntax layer. Whether the two faces are one sheet is recorded once per spread, in `physical_observations` (f12–f13 is the centre of the gathering and a single sheet; f16–f17 spans two sheets).

### 2. Sixteen face corrections in f10–f11 (identifiers kept)

The sixteen units from `tu_f11_celui` to `tu_f11_folie` are printed on f10, not f11. Only `tu_f11_n_abolira` is on f11. The error dates from the first draft of 2026-07-18. The editor ruled the correction on 2026-09-26 and checked it again against the copy on 2026-11-24.

A misrecorded face is a correction of an observation, not a change of identity, so **the identifiers do not change**. Each correction is a `revision_history` entry (`field: face`, `before: f11`, `after: f10`). The `f11` in these identifiers now records the face at the time of issue, not the current face.

This differs from the earlier handling at f14–f15 (2026-07-20, before publication), where a face misattribution was resolved by retiring identifiers. That entry is kept as it was.

### 3. Validator

- Check 3 now reads: *the id's face prefix matches `face`, or the face corrections form an unbroken chain from the prefix to the current face.* A record of correction is not enough by itself. The chain must start at the identifier's face and end at the current face.
- Check 21 is new: *retired ids in `deprecated_ids` are not duplicated, do not remain in `units`, and every `superseded_by` resolves to a current id.* The earlier checks keep their numbers.

### 4. Other corrections (no unit fields affected)

- f6–f7: the blank between `par` and `avance retombée…` had been given as ≈67 mm, a value converted from the photograph. The editor's ruler measurement is 8 cm (4 cm on each side of the gutter). The record now gives the ruler value; the pixel values remain as reference.
- `typography_scale.json`: the proposed class-3 italic anchor moves from `tu_f12_une_insinuation_simple` to its left successor `tu_f12_une_insinuation`. Its size basis was the whole spread (`census_uniform`), so the basis is unchanged.
- `proof_states.json`: a dated note records that the "face f11" quoted for Fiançailles in 2026-08 was later corrected.

## What did not change

- The citation scheme (v1.0, frozen 2026-07-17).
- 210 identifiers, including the sixteen whose face was corrected.
- The text of the poem itself, and the type class and style of every fragment. (The extent of seven units changed; that is the change.)
- The eight records not listed above. Their `schema_version` stays `0.3`, which records the schema they were written under. The four records revised here carry `0.4`.

## If you cite level-0

- A citation pinned to v1.1 (`ucd:v1.1://unit/…`) still refers to the v1.1 state. That state is the repository release 1.0.2.
- A retired identifier remains valid. Look it up in `deprecated_ids` and follow `superseded_by`. It now leads to two units.
- New citations should pin v2.0: `ucd:v2.0://unit/tu_f12_une_insinuation`.

## If you build a layer

- Declare `"depends_on": { "level0": ">=2.0.0" }`.
- A layer written against 1.x must be re-validated. Claims attached to the seven retired identifiers must be re-attached to one or both successors. Claims that rely on the face of the sixteen corrected f10–f11 units should be re-checked.
- No layer had been published before 2.0.0.
